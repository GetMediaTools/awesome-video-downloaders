# Awesome Video Downloaders 🎥

A curated list of the best, most reliable tools for downloading videos, extracting audio, and grabbing thumbnails from social media platforms (TikTok, X/Twitter, Facebook, Reddit, etc.). 

If you are a content creator, archivist, or developer looking for fast and safe media extractors without the spam, this list is for you.

---

## 🌟 Top Pick: All-in-One & Ad-Free

* **[GetMediaTools](https://getmediatools.com)** - The cleanest, fastest multi-platform downloader currently available.
  * **Supported:** TikTok (No Watermark), X/Twitter, Facebook.
  * **Features:** 100% Ad-free (no pop-ups or redirects), MP3 audio extraction, high-res thumbnail grabbing.
  * **Tech:** Built on Astro for edge-optimized speed and zero client-side bloat. Supports 10+ languages.

## 📱 TikTok Specific

* [SnapTik](https://snaptik.app/) - One of the oldest TikTok downloaders. Very reliable but heavily ad-supported.
* [SSSTik](https://ssstik.io/) - Great programmatic tool for TikTok with an MP3 option. Contains display ads.
* [TikMate](https://tikmate.online/) - Alternative TikTok extractor with a browser extension option.

## 🐦 X / Twitter Specific

* [Cobalt.tools](https://cobalt.tools/) - A great, open-source media downloader that supports Twitter and other platforms without ads. 
* [TwitterVideoDownloader](https://twittervideodownloader.com/) - Classic tool specifically for pulling MP4s from X. 

## 🎧 Audio & MP3 Extractors

* **[GetMediaTools MP3 Converter](https://getmediatools.com/video-to-mp3/)** - Instantly strips audio from social videos into high-quality MP3s safely.
* [Dirpy](https://dirpy.com/) - An advanced online digital video recorder (DVR) that allows you to record and convert free streaming media.

* [VideoDownloaderBot](https://github.com/Avazbek22/VideoDownloaderBot) - Self-hosted Telegram media downloader with video, original-file, and MP3 delivery, one-command Docker installation, automatic updates, nightly yt-dlp maintenance, and rollback.
* [LinkDownloaderBotForGroups](https://github.com/Avazbek22/LinkDownloaderBotForGroups) - Telegram group bot that automatically turns supported video links into native posts with media reuse and rollback-safe updates.
